{{- if eq .Values.mode "redis" }}

Storage mode redis: everything is kept in one Redis, with no PostgreSQL. The Secret also needs
storage.redis_url and storage.key (no storage.postgres_dsn: the proxy refuses one in this mode).
Redis must run with appendonly yes and maxmemory-policy noeviction on a persistent volume, or the
replicas refuse to start; back up its data folder. Each replica keeps its own data claim
(data-{{ $f }}-N): keep them, they let a restarted replica notice a Redis that lost its data.
