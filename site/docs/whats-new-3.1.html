What's new in 3.1 #

Maple Harness 3.1 is a tidy-up after the big move, eh: a setup page you can find your way around, documentation with a proper map, a /help that finds anything, more of the session on your phone, and a new way to share one model over several of your own servers. Nothing to do on one computer: update, restart the proxy and carry on. This page is the tour; each card opens the guide.

The short version

In plain words #

  • The setup page is easier to find your way around. A sidebar of short pages in four groups, a search for any page or setting, and old bookmarks lead to the right place.
  • The documentation has a map. A sidebar grouped by topic, a search window, and a card for every section on the docs home.
  • /help finds anything. Every command, key, setting and tool of a session in one dialog you can search.
  • Your phone sees more. Tap a task, an agent or a background command to see its details, on small phones and folded covers too.
  • Several servers of one model. A model group can send each conversation to the server that already has its prompt in its cache.
  • /remote copes without Tailscale. It asks which address your phone should use, and recommends your local network when Tailscale is not available.

The setup page #

  • A sidebar of short pages The menu is a sidebar in groups: Watch for what is happening, Configure for what you change, Access for phones, tokens and the page password, System for storage, addresses and every setting. Each page lists its sections under its name.
  • Find anything Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) or / from anywhere finds a page, a card or a setting by a word, and takes you to it.
  • One place per setting Each setting lives on one card; All settings lists every one of them, links to its card, and holds the few without one.
  • Old links keep working Bookmarks and links from 2.x and 3.0 open the page and card that hold them now. What moved lists the names that changed.

The documentation site #

  • A grouped sidebar Twelve folding sections, the page you are on highlighted and its section open; previous and next links say when they lead into another section.
  • A search window Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) or / opens it, full screen on a phone: pages first, then sections, each with where it is. It works offline.
  • A section map Browse by section on the docs home has a card for every section, with its first pages.

The agent #

  • The new /help Everything the session has, in sections (Getting started, Conversation, Agents & lineup, Settings, Keys and more). Type to search commands, keys, settings and tools at once, best match first (wktr finds /worktree); Enter runs a command or opens a setting. /help <words> opens it already searched.

Remote control #

  • Details on the phone Tap a task in the lineup for who has it, what it waits for and unblocks, the check that must pass, its files and its note. Background commands and monitors are listed with the agents, with what they printed; an agent's details open on a phone too. A device that drives can reassign or stop; a watch-only device reads only.
  • Folded covers and small phones On a folded cover's glance view, the task in progress opens its details, and the agents, the lineup and background commands are one tap away. A detail's actions stay in a bar at the bottom, in reach of one thumb.
  • Which address for your phone /remote asks which address your first device will use before it shows the QR code, and recommends your Tailscale name when Tailscale is signed in.
  • Without Tailscale When Tailscale is not installed, not running, signed out or not answering, /remote stops waiting after a few seconds and recommends your local network. A Tailscale address is offered lower down, marked, with the reason and how to get Tailscale back (tailscale up, then /remote pair --via tailscale).

The proxy #

  • Cache-aware model groups For the same model on several servers of your own, strategy: cache-aware sends each conversation to the server expected to hold the start of its prompt, and lets a quieter server take it when that one is clearly busier. Replicas of the proxy agree without sharing anything.
  • Copilot models on the Responses API A Copilot model served only on Copilot's Responses endpoint (the mai-code-2 models) is sent there, so Copilot Auto and Copilot Fusion no longer fail when Copilot picks one. A model your plan does not allow is passed over for another at once. Arrived in 3.0.2.

Not verified yet #

Note

Cache-aware model groups were checked with the proxy's own tests and a simulation, not yet against a real vLLM, SGLang or llama.cpp server, and what the proxy knows about each server's cache is an estimate. The phone's detail views on folded covers and small phones were tried in emulated screen sizes, not on real devices.

Upgrading #

  • Install maple-harness update, or the installer again, then restart the proxy.
