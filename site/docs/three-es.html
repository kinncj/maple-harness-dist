    Settings by goal #

    On this page
    1. I want to spend less on long runs
    2. I want the work to stay as good
    3. I want to stop runaways
    4. I want results I can rely on

    Maple Harness aims at three things at once: to be efficient (fewer tokens for the same work), efficacious (the work stays as good, and defaults are safe) and effective (whole tasks succeed and waste stops early). This page maps a goal to the settings and commands that serve it. It makes no promise about your bill: results vary from run to run and task to task, so measure your own, and turn off anything that does not help on your work. Every setting listed has an off switch.

    I want to spend less on long runs #

    Do thisWhereTrade-off
    Keep one model for a long task instead of switching/model <name>, or /model auto (it keeps your model when it fits)A fixed model may be more than a small job needs
    See where the money goes/cost, the setup page's Cache card, maple-proxy cacheNone
    Keep the provider's cache workingproviders[].prompt_cache (on by default), prefix_stabilizer (on by default)None for the defaults
    Trim old tool output in very long conversationscontext_trim on the Guards card (off by default)The model may ask for trimmed output again
    Keep noisy searches out of the main conversation@name <task> or /agent, with a subagentA subagent starts with only the task you give it
    Stop a stuck run earlyloop_guard.mode (on by default)It may end a run you would have let continue
    Shorten long failing logstool_output.failed_max_lines (on by default)The model sees less of a long log
    Cap what a provider can cost per daybudgets[] on the Budgets cardRequests are refused, or fall back, when reached
    Start fresh tasks in fresh sessions/clear, or a new sessionYou lose the earlier context

    I want the work to stay as good #

    Do thisWhereTrade-off
    Let the compiler check the model's editslsp (on by default), /lsp to see what is runningThe first edit starts the server in the background
    Keep the safe defaultspermission.mode: ask, sandbox.mode, file_policy.*More questions, fewer surprises
    Prefer the tools that show a diffbash_file_writes: warn (default) or denydeny refuses shell writes
    Plan before changing anything/planAn extra step
    Use a capable model for hard work/model, auto_model_mapCosts more per turn
    Keep automatic choice away from models that decline long tasksauto_avoidA pattern may hide a model you would have liked
    Check your setupmaple-harness doctorNone

    I want to stop runaways #

    Do thisWhereTrade-off
    End a turn that keeps failing the same way, with a summaryloop_guard.mode: stop, loop_guard.stop_afterSee above
    Cut a reply that repeats itselfdegenerate_guard (the repeat guard) on the proxy's Guards cardA repetitive answer could be cut short
    Refuse or trim a request bigger than the model's windowcontext_guardtruncate forgets old turns
    Cap the turns a subagent may takesubagents.max_turnsA long job may need more
    Limit how fast a client may call the proxyserver.rate_limit.*Bursts are slowed
    Move to another model when one runs outmodel_fallbacksThe fallback may be different or weaker

    I want results I can rely on #

    Do thisWhereTrade-off
    Get told when a session needs younotifications.*, maple-harness notify testNone
    Resume where you left off--continue, --resume, /sessionsOld sessions take disk; cleanup removes them
    Keep a checklist for a big requestledger: auto, /ledgerOne extra model call
    Make the model carry on when it stops earlyon by default; disable_auto_continue turns it offNone for the default
    Check a change against your own tasksmaple-harness --prompt with --json-summary, repeated with a setting on and offIt costs the runs you make
