Which setting for which goal #

Maple Harness aims at three things at once: to be efficient (fewer tokens for the same work), efficacious (the work stays as good, and defaults are safe) and effective (whole tasks succeed and waste stops early). This page maps a goal to the settings and commands that serve it. It makes no promise about your bill: results vary from run to run and task to task, so measure your own, and turn off anything that does not help on your work. Every setting listed has an off switch.

I want to spend less on long runs #

Keep one model for a long task instead of switching/model <name>, or /model auto (it keeps your model when it fits)A fixed model may be more than a small job needs
See where the money goes/cost, the setup page's Cache card, maple-proxy cacheNone
Keep the provider's cache workingproviders[].prompt_cache (on by default), prefix_stabilizer (on by default)None for the defaults
Trim old tool output in very long conversationscontext_trim on the Guards card (off by default)The model may ask for trimmed output again
Keep noisy searches out of the main conversation@name <task> or /agent, with a subagentA subagent starts with only the task you give it
Stop a stuck run earlyloop_guard.mode (on by default)It may end a run you would have let continue
Shorten long failing logstool_output.failed_max_lines (on by default)The model sees less of a long log
Cap what a provider can cost per daybudgets[] on the Budgets cardRequests are refused, or fall back, when reached
Start fresh tasks in fresh sessions/clear, or a new sessionYou lose the earlier context

Do thisWhereTrade-off
